## Research interests

Computational materials thermodynamics and phase diagrams; predictive synthesis of inorganic materials; inverse materials design; materials informatics; AI for accelerating materials from discovery to deployment.

## Research experience

**Research Assistant, Musgrave Group, University of Colorado Boulder, 2021–2026**

- Developed a data-driven framework to screen hybrid cathode materials for metal-based batteries ([<em>J. Mater. Chem. A</em> 2025](/publications/#p10)).
- Identified hafnium perovskite solid electrolytes stable against lithium metal by first-principles stability analysis ([<em>EES Batteries</em> 2025](/publications/#p11)).
- Computed the temperature-, pressure- and chemical-potential-dependent stability of 1,040 ordered MXenes with r²SCAN DFT ([arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14687), 2026).
- Performed DFT for experimental collaborators on heterostructures, solid electrolytes and CO₂ reduction ([<em>PRX Energy</em>](/publications/#p15), [<em>Energy Storage Mater.</em>](/publications/#p13), [<em>Appl. Catal. A</em>](/publications/#p12), 2026).

**Research Assistant, Energy Materials Laboratory, The American University in Cairo, 2015–2021**

- Modeled quantum capacitance and alkali-ion intercalation in MoS₂ and carbon electrodes for supercapacitors ([<em>Mater. Today Energy</em>](/publications/#p4), [<em>Mater. Adv.</em>](/publications/#p5), [<em>Chem. Commun.</em>](/publications/#p6), 2021).
- Showed that carbon defects narrow the band gap of m-ZrO₂ for photoelectrochemical water splitting ([<em>Int. J. Hydrogen Energy</em> 2021](/publications/#p7)).
